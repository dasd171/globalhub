# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and inclusive across languages and cultures, assume good faith, give constructive feedback, and do not harass anyone. Commercial disputes between buyers and suppliers using a GlobalHub node are handled by that node's operator, not in this repository.

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers (configure a contact address before publishing). Maintainers may remove content and ban participants who violate this code.
